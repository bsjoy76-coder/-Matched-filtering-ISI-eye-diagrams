Expected: at high SNR with a correctly compensated sampling instant and no multipath, the eye should be wide open and BER near zero. Reducing SNR fills the eye with noise; a nonzero sampling offset moves the sampling instant off the eye's center, raising BER even without noise; enabling multipath ISI closes the eye vertically because delayed echoes of neighboring symbols add onto the current sample.
